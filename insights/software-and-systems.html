Every Quarri insight on software and systems.

What should you look for in lumber ERP software in 2026?Beyond features: how it records your awkward transactions, and whether your data comes out cleanly. Test both in the demo with your own examples.What kinds of sawmill software are there, and which do you need?Four kinds, each holding a different record: optimisers, planning, ERP and maintenance. What you need depends on which record your questions need.What does the forestry software market look like in 2026?Published size estimates differ by a factor of two and don't always add up. What matters is whether the tools' links reach tickets, contracts and accounts.What is the difference between forestry management software and forestry inventory software?Inventory software estimates what is on the ground at a date, with error. Management software records what is done. Check the handoff keeps date and error.What can lumber inventory management software tell you, and what can't it?It tells you what the records say, by item and location. It can't tell you what is in the yard, or which way the records are wrong. A count shows both.Why does sawmill accounting software rarely match the mill?Logs enter by scale and lumber leaves by tally, and the ratio moves with the logs. A standard factor, or one average, hides where margin comes from.What can sawmill optimisation software do, and what can't it?It finds the highest-value way to cut each log against a price file. It can't tell whether the prices are current or whether anyone ordered the product.What is timber inventory software?Software that turns plot and tree measurements into estimates of standing volume, products and value. The good kind reports how uncertain each estimate is.What does lumber yard ERP software need to handle?Units of measure, stored once on each item so every module agrees. Every lumber ERP claims it; the test is where conversions live, and whether they drift.Can you get data out of an old lumber ERP with no API?Usually, yes. The reliable routes are the database and the reports the system already prints. Driving its screens with bots is the least reliable route.Why do software vendors charge for data access?Partly real costs, partly because access is where switching costs live. The 2024 cloud exit-fee waivers show how such fees behave when rules change.Should a forest products company build its own data platform or buy one?Buy the plumbing, as almost everyone does. The decision is who builds, owns and maintains the timber meaning: units, tallies, keys and rules.What is the difference between an ERP, a WMS and a data platform?They record the same stock at different grains. The WMS knows where it is, the ERP what it is worth, and a data platform keeps both over time.How do scale house systems and ERPs share data?Tickets pass from scale to ERP as weight, and the ERP turns weight into volume and money with a factor. The keys and the factor are where it breaks.What doesn't a converter's ERP tell you?For a packaging converter costing at standard: the grade really run, trim and set-up waste by order, and the cost of serving each customer.How do you evaluate a data platform for a timber business?On your own data, with questions whose answers you already know. Score it exactly, include questions it should refuse, and test how it handles change.What should an AI vendor tell you about your data before you sign?What it reads and with whose permissions, where it is kept, what it retains and for how long, who else touches it, and how you get it back.Lumber ERP alternatives for mid-size distributorsBefore choosing a new ERP, check the problem is the ERP. If it is reporting and scattered data, a data layer over the current one avoids a migration.
