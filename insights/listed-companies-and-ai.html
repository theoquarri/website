Every Quarri insight on listed companies and ai.

How is West Fraser using AI and data?Its filings name no AI project, but outside them it is piloting autonomous log hauling and hiring AI developers, while unifying legacy IT platforms.How is UFP Industries using AI?It says it is investing in AI and machine learning but names no project or figure. The capital it describes and sizes goes into automation.How is Louisiana-Pacific using technology?Its 10-K shows product technology, a published OEE metric by business, and AI in standard risk language as "in the early phases". Siding OEE rose in 2026.How is Stora Enso using AI?In its forests, for planning and monitoring: a digital forest twin in its 2024 and 2025 reports, with biodiversity the newest aim. Partners show more.How is UPM using AI?A company-wide AI programme, pilots in orders and invoices, a field assistant used by hundreds of foresters, and machine vision in pulp mills.How is Holmen using data?Above all to value its forest: a regression on property sales, three methods each within 1.2% of the weighted value, and a 2019 inventory rolled forward.How is SCA using technology in forestry?Laser scanning and harvester data combined with AI into a digital twin for harvest planning, an EU single-tree project, and AI log scanning at a sawmill.How is Mondi using AI?In its reports, mainly as a board topic focused on production. Outside them, a university AI tool it uses to choose paper grades, and AI in HR since 2019.How is International Paper using AI?Its 10-K treats AI only as a risk; a vendor story says it runs ML on mill sensor data.
