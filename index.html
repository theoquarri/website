01

Live connection

Every system you already run, brought into one live layer: your ERP, mill system, on-prem database and the spreadsheets your team actually trusts. Connected into leading models to build customised outputs.

  • No migration project and no rip-out
  • A live sync, not a one-off export
  • New sources added whenever you need them

One deployment syncs your ERP daily alongside weighbridge data, scanned authority invoices and live weather.

02

AI-powered document extraction

Scale tickets, field tallies, delivery dockets, supplier invoices and contracts. Handwritten, scanned or buried in a legacy export, read and turned into structured, queryable data.

  • Every value traceable back to the page it came from
  • Human in the loop for exceptions
  • Replayable, auditable, cheap and works at scale

03

Automated reconciliation

Way tickets, operational data sets, accounting ledgers and invoices reconciled, with exceptions flagged.

  • Variances flagged and aged rather than absorbed
  • Quick issue resolution
  • Entries posted straight into your finance system (coming soon)

Caught by matching at bill-of-lading level, not by comparing totals.

04

Dynamic forecasting

Your own history, overlaid with the outside world. Volume, demand and price modelled against live weather and price inflation.

  • Confidence bands, not single-point guesses
  • Each day rated for operability before crews are committed
  • External data piped in live

Bottleneck detection

See where throughput actually goes. Yield, recovery and downtime across every stage, so the real constraint is managed.

  • Recovery and quality tracked by line, shift and species
  • Bottlenecks identified from your own data
  • Drift helps forward manage

Early warning of key bottlenecks in the process line.

Smart Scheduler

The order book sequenced against what the plant can actually do, drawing oldest stock first, with reorder points and supplier lead times live.

  • Reorder flags raised before you run short
  • Supplier lead times re-rated from what actually happened
  • Dead and excess stock surfaced against months of supply

AI powered reordering, tailored to your workflows, inputs and outputs, flagging reorders so you hit every PO.

Margin intelligence

Input and output prices, both benchmarked. Every purchase line compared against your own like-for-like buying, and every quote priced against where the market actually is.

  • Benchmark against internal data and CPI
  • Sales trends, margin floors, and account and SKU level margin
  • Bankable savings from supplier switching and requote prompts

// Why Quarri

Purpose-built for timber. Not adapted to it.

Generic platforms treat species, grades and board feet as a data-cleaning problem. We treat them as the business.

Built for the industry

Tonnes and cubic metres upstream, board feet and MBF downstream, with conversions, species codes, grades and random lengths already understood rather than configured in later.

AI-native, deterministic where it counts

The matching, reconciling and converting runs as encoded logic, not model guesswork. AI does the reasoning on top, so there are no hallucinated numbers in front of your board.

One connected layer

Your ERP, mill system and spreadsheets stay where they are. Quarri governs a layer over them, so you are live in weeks rather than committed to a five-year replacement.
