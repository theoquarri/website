Your data is safe

Quarri never trains models on your data. What Claude reads from Quarri is handled under your own Claude plan (see below).
Your own database. Your company gets its own database, separate from every other customer's.
Encrypted everywhere. AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2 or later in transit. Keys and passwords are stored encrypted and never pass through the chat.
Where it is hosted. Free tier workspaces are hosted in the US East region. Enterprise customers can choose US or EU hosting.
Every action logged. Queries, sign-ins and admin changes are recorded, and you can have the log on request.
Your Claude account: on a personal Claude plan, turn off "Help improve Claude" in Settings, Privacy. Team and Enterprise plans have it off already.

More on our security page.

// 02

What Quarri is

Quarri keeps a company's operational data in one place, organised so that questions about it get the same answer every time. It is built for forestry, lumber, wood products and paper converting. People use it through Claude: they ask questions, build dashboards and small apps, and set up alerts and scheduled emails, all by asking. Each company has its own workspace.

First time? Read on from First time: before you start. Used Quarri before? Skip to Coming back: things to say.

// 03

First time: before you start

Step 1Sign up at app.quarri.ai/signup: continue with Google, Microsoft or your email. The free tier has no time limit and includes one data connector you set up yourself in Claude, two users (anyone a dashboard is shared with counts as one), 1 GB of data, one scheduled job and five emails a week. A free workspace comes with ready-made data to try things on: a demo sawmill and the log market it buys against.
Step 2Add Quarri to Claude. The quickest way is the Add Quarri to Claude button on the page you land on. By hand: in Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, choose Add custom connector, name it Quarri and paste https://app.quarri.ai/mcp. Sign in when asked.
  • Claude Desktop: add it on the web as above, then fully quit and reopen the desktop app. You can also add it inside the desktop app under Customize, Connectors, Add, Custom connector.
  • Team or Enterprise plan: an owner adds Quarri once under Organization settings, Connectors. Each member then connects it with their own Quarri sign-in.
  • Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http quarri https://app.quarri.ai/mcp
Step 3Start. In a new chat, type Get started with Quarri.

Getting the most from your Claude plan

  • Ask and explore in the chat. Questions, summaries and small changes cost little.
  • Ask for the change, not the page. "Show the last 12 months" is a small change Claude makes to its copy of the page in Claude Code; it never needs to read the whole page into the chat.
  • Build in Claude Code. Asking questions is fine anywhere; building dashboards, apps and connectors is code, and Claude Code works on a copy on your computer and uploads it in one step. In the chat the whole page travels through the conversation, which uses up your plan quickly.
  • Say what you want before Claude builds. The names your team uses, the tabs, the date range. Changing them afterwards costs more than getting them right first.
  • If something fails twice, stop and ask Claude why before trying again. Repeating a failing step repeats its cost. If Quarri can't do it yet, ask the Quarri team.
