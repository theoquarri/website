// Your working cycle

Four phases, from the document to the board pack.

Finance in an industrial business is mostly the work of proving things. Here is where that work breaks down, and what changes when the proving happens once rather than every month.

01Capture & Reconcile02Close & Control03Margin & Mix04Cash & Forecast
01

Capture & Reconcile

Invoice · ticket · GRN · ledger

Most of the evidence finance needs arrives as paper or PDF, and somebody keys it. The systems it has to agree with were never designed to talk to each other, so the reconciliation is done by eye, on volume, once a month. What that method finds is the differences big enough to notice, which is not the same as the differences that matter.

// Where Quarri works

Quarri reads the documents as they arrive and matches them to the ledger line by line, so a difference is found by the process rather than by somebody being thorough that week.

  • Invoices, tickets and GRNs read, not retyped
  • Purchase, receipt and invoice matched per line
  • Differences surfaced rather than absorbed
  • Every figure traceable back to its document
Document captureSystems reconciliationAudit trail
// Documents · structuredpaper in

Documents read off the page and matched to the ledger, so the audit trail is a by-product of the process rather than a separate job.

02

Close & Control

Accrue · reconcile · post · close

The close runs on a workbook that a small number of people understand, and the rules inside it live in cells rather than anywhere documented. It works, until the person who built it is away, or the business changes shape, or somebody asks why a number moved. Then the answer takes days to assemble and rests on the same workbook nobody can check.

// Where Quarri works

Quarri drafts the accrual from live data and holds the rules where your team can read and edit them, so the close stops depending on one workbook and one person.

  • Accruals drafted from live data each cycle
  • Rate tables and mappings held openly, not in cells
  • Unmatched items aged in view, never absorbed
  • Postings written back as a file finance can use
Accrual automationRules you can editClose controls
// Accrual against invoicegap $0.01

One release cycle reconciled at document level, surfacing an over-accrual credit that a monthly comparison would have netted away.

03

Margin & Mix

Product · customer · branch · mix

Group margin is a number everybody has and nobody can act on. Underneath it sit customers bought at one price and served at another, products that lost money quietly for a year, and internal transfers that make a branch look like a business. Reported monthly and in aggregate, a mix problem and a rate problem look identical.

// Where Quarri works

Quarri decomposes margin into customer, product and rate effects, so a compression you can fix is told apart from one you merely have to explain.

  • Margin split by customer, product, branch and rate
  • Internal transfers excluded from trading margin
  • Duplicate customer identities merged before reporting
  • Retention and cohort movement measured, not assumed
Margin by customerMargin by productMix decomposition
// Margin bridgemix, not rate

At one manufacturer, inter-branch transfers had turned a healthy lifetime margin into a reported loss until they were separated out.

04

Cash & Forecast

AR · AP · cash · forecast

Cash is the number the board asks about and the one assembled last. Receivables age in one system, commitments to suppliers in another, and the stock in between was bought with money you can no longer use. Each is knowable; together they are a week of work, which is why the forecast is usually a shape rather than a position.

// Where Quarri works

Quarri holds receivables, commitments and stock value in one place, so a cash position is read rather than rebuilt.

  • Receivables aged by customer and by limit
  • Credit exposure flagged against agreed terms
  • Cash tied up in stock, valued per SKU
  • Board-ready views exported, not rebuilt

// In practice

A lumber and millwork manufacturer

// Before

Group margin was reported monthly from a workbook nobody outside finance could check. Purchasing ran across a long tail of suppliers with no like-for-like comparison, and the same customer appeared under several spellings.

// With Quarri

Quarri matched purchase, receipt and invoice line by line, tracing a unit-of-measure error that had inflated purchase spend for years and surfacing a six-figure procurement saving. Separating inter-branch transfers from trading activity turned a reported loss back into a healthy lifetime margin. The rebuilt reporting was checked against the platform total and agreed to $126 on over $26M, with zero arithmetic failures across 267 independent checks. Read the case study.
