These Free Tier Terms are an agreement between the organisation you sign up for (the "Customer", "you") and Quarri AI Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal office at 390 NE 191st St STE 18580, Miami, FL 33179 ("Quarri", "we"). This is the same contracting entity as the MSA.

1.2 How you accept. By continuing past the sign-in screen you accept these Free Tier Terms on behalf of your organisation, and you confirm you have authority to do so. If you do not have that authority, do not continue.

1.3 Business use only. The free tier is for organisations using Quarri in their business. It is not offered to consumers. If a workspace is not used by an organisation in its business, we may close it under section 7.1.

1.4 How these terms fit with our other terms.

  • These Free Tier Terms govern the free tier.

  • If your organisation signs a Master Services and Subscription Agreement with us (an "MSA"), the MSA replaces these Free Tier Terms (section 11).

2. The free tier

2.1 The free tier gives your organisation one Quarri workspace, free of charge, with no time limit. It includes one data connector, which you set up yourself in Claude. Further connectors, and any work by the Quarri team, are on the Enterprise plan under an MSA (section 11).

2.2 The free tier comes with no service level:

  • No service level applies.
  • The uptime and recovery figures on our security page do not apply.
  • The service levels in our customer agreements do not apply.

2.3 One free workspace per organisation. We may decline a sign-up. Suspension and closure are covered in section 7.1.

2.4 You may invite people in your organisation to the workspace ("Authorised Users"). You are responsible for their use of it, and for keeping sign-in credentials confidential.

3. Your data

3.1 Definitions. These follow the MSA definitions, so that nothing changes if you move to a paid plan.

  • "Free Tier Data" means all data, information and content submitted, uploaded or otherwise transmitted by you or an Authorised User through the Platform on the free tier. It includes any data extracted from your connected systems.
  • "Free Tier Tools" means any custom tools, skills, workflows, automations, dashboards, queries or configurations that you or your Authorised Users create or save in the workspace.

3.2 Your data is yours.

  • You own your Free Tier Data and your Free Tier Tools.
  • You give us a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use, process and display Free Tier Data only as needed to provide the free tier to you.
  • We do not sell, share or disclose Free Tier Data to third parties, except as needed to provide the free tier or as required by law.
  • We do not use Free Tier Data to train AI models. (MSA 11.1 is narrower here: see open point 4.)

3.3 Demo data. If you choose demo data, your workspace starts with public price statistics from their official publishers, with each publisher's attribution. It contains no customer data.

3.4 Connecting your own systems.

  • Connect only data that you are entitled to share with a service provider.
  • Connector credentials are entered through a one-time link and stored encrypted.

3.5 No personal data in the workspace. This follows MSA 11.3.

  • The free tier is designed for business and operational data. It is not designed for personal data (information about an identified or identifiable person).
  • You agree not to put personal data into the workspace. You will use reasonable efforts to exclude, remove or anonymise it before connecting a source.
  • For Free Tier Data, we do not act as a processor of personal data, and no data processing agreement is offered on the free tier.
  • If personal data is nonetheless in Free Tier Data, we will treat it as your Confidential Information and protect it as described in section 3.6.
  • If you need Quarri to process personal data, talk to us about an MSA with data processing terms.
  • Personal information about you and your Authorised Users as account holders (names, work email addresses, sign-in and usage records) is handled under our Privacy Policy. For that information, Quarri is the controller.

3.6 Security. We protect Free Tier Data with the measures described on our security page, which include:

  • a separate database for each customer;
  • AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.2 or later in transit;
  • role-based access and audit logging;
  • each sign-in reaching only the databases that user has been given.

Hosting region. Free tier workspaces are hosted in the United States, in the US East region. There is no choice of region on the free tier; the Enterprise plan offers a US or EU region.

3.7 Aggregated statistics, methods and tools. This follows MSA 11.4 and 12.5.

  • We may compile anonymised statistics about use of the Platform. They do not identify you or your data.
  • Tools, techniques and methods we develop remain ours, provided we do not disclose your Free Tier Data or Confidential Information in doing so.

4. Using Quarri through Claude

4.1 You use Quarri through Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, by adding Quarri as a connector. Your use of Claude is under your own agreement with Anthropic.

4.2 What Claude sees. When you ask Claude a question, Claude calls Quarri's tools. What those tools return, which can include Free Tier Data, goes into your conversation with Claude. Anthropic handles it under your Claude plan and Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not under these Free Tier Terms.

  • Anthropic's privacy policy for individual accounts says conversation content, including content from connected services, may be used to train its models unless you opt out in your settings.
  • Check your organisation's Claude plan and settings before you connect data you consider sensitive.
  • Quarri does not control Claude. For this flow, Anthropic is your provider, not Quarri's subcontractor.

Separately, Quarri's search features use OpenAI models run by Microsoft Azure, through our data platform provider, to turn text into search embeddings. These are processed in the data platform's region.

4.3 Quarri is not Anthropic, and Anthropic is not a party to these terms.

4.4 AI output. Answers and analysis produced with AI can be wrong. Review them before relying on them for material decisions. Quarri does not give financial, legal or investment advice.

7. When the free tier ends, and your data

7.1 The free tier has no fixed end date. It continues until:

  • (a) your organisation moves to a paid plan (section 11);
  • (b) you close the workspace, which you can do at any time;
  • (c) we close it, on 30 days' notice to the email address used at sign-up;
  • (d) we close it, on 30 days' notice, after 6 months in which nobody has signed in; or
  • (e) we suspend or close it straight away under section 5.2, or where it presents a security risk.

7.2 If you move to a paid plan, your workspace carries on unchanged (section 11).

7.3 If the free tier ends without a move to a paid plan (any of 7.1(b) to (e)):

  • (a) Scheduled jobs (connector refreshes, alerts, scheduled emails) stop when the free tier ends.
  • (b) The workspace stays available read-only for 30 days, so you can export your Free Tier Data and Free Tier Tools. This does not apply where we close it under 7.1(e).
  • (c) After that, we delete the workspace and its Free Tier Data within 7 days.
  • (d) Deleted data leaves our rolling backups within 30 days.

7.4 You can ask us to delete your workspace and Free Tier Data at any time. We will confirm in writing when it is done, within 14 days of your request.

7.5 Operational records, such as logs of tool calls and sign-in events, are kept for security and support as described in our Privacy Policy. The platform log is kept for 90 days.
